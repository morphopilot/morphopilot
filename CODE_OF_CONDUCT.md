# Community expectations

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique technical decisions, explain evidence, and assume that people may be unfamiliar with your research domain. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate and explainable. Concerns may be reported to a repository maintainer using a private contact method listed on their public GitHub profile; do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.
